# Non-Abelian vortex collisions

Two filaments touch. As their far ends draw apart, a short crosspiece stretches between two moving junctions. One contact has changed what counts as a connected object. A silhouette can carry the whole event.

**Structure.** Loops around line defects in an ordered medium carry elements of the fundamental group of the order-parameter space, with a common base point for comparing charges. For noncommuting charges, the topology obstructs an uncomplicated crossing. In the cyclic spin-2 condensate model studied below, collisions produce a stable connecting rung. The result concerns particular defect charges and encounters.

**Clock and coupling.** Connectivity records an event. It gives a before and after with little sense of elapsed time. The coupling comes from both lines belonging to one field that must remain topologically consistent away from their cores. If the charges themselves are invisible, the crossbar is the first legible evidence that the two filaments carried different information.

**Source.** Kobayashi, Kawaguchi, Nitta and Ueda (2009) give the topological argument and numerical collisions. [Collision Dynamics and Rung Formation of Non-Abelian Vortices](https://arxiv.org/abs/0810.5441)

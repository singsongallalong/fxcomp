# Dynamics for visual effects

A field guide to motion that is easy to recognize and difficult to choreograph by hand. The notes describe mathematical mechanisms and give interpretations for short, precomputed effects. The visual readings are conjectures about what a viewer could perceive. The linked papers establish the underlying phenomena.

## Two ways into the collection

- [Irreversibility and visual clocks](maps/irreversibility.md) asks where visible order goes, what could bring it back, and when a quantity can actually serve as a clock.
- [Ways to couple simple systems](maps/composition.md) follows the influence from one component to another. Similar pictures can arise from interference, transport, energy exchange, a shared constraint, or a change in stability.

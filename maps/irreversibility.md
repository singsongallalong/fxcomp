# Irreversibility and visual clocks

A fan of repelling particles can spread out in three senses. Its spatial footprint can grow, its outline can become rounder, and its microscopic state can become harder to infer from an image. These have different clocks. In a Hamiltonian model the full phase-space flow preserves volume even as the position marginal spreads. In an overdamped gradient model the interaction energy falls. If the particles fly apart in nearly parallel streaks, a roomy cloud can retain a remarkably accurate portrait of its initial anisotropy.

An entropy becomes a clock only after specifying the state, the observation and the dynamics. A strictly monotone functional orders points on a relaxing trajectory. A weakly monotone one permits plateaus. Two states with the same value can have quite different shapes and different futures. Ensemble entropy, spatial density entropy, phase coherence and interfacial free energy each answer a different question. Even within free expansion, changing the chosen macrovariables can alter the observed time course. [Chakraborti et al., entropy growth during free expansion](https://arxiv.org/abs/2109.07742)

## Where the visible order goes

Reversing a stirring motion afterwards cannot restore the dissipated contrast. [Stam](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0019995859903481) |
| Free-energy relaxation and coarsening | Interfacial cost falls in gradient-flow models. A typical domain size can increase statistically. | Boundaries stay sharp while whole islands disappear. ## Three ways the clocks can disagree

**Dephasing with almost no change in local brightness.** For initially aligned, independent oscillators with frequency law $g$, the complex order parameter is $Z(t)=\int e^{i\omega t}g(\omega)\,d\omega$. Its magnitude is the coherence visible in a global pulse. A Gaussian spread of frequencies gives smooth loss of coherence. Two narrow, separated frequency bands can give repeated beats under a gradually decaying envelope. The frequency distribution alone can change the character of the fade. In a [chimera](../ideas/chimera-states.md) coupling makes even the *location* of phase locking an outcome.

**Mixing that creates its own route to dissipation.** For an incompressibly advected passive scalar in a periodic domain, diffusion gives $\tfrac12\,d\|c-\bar c\|_2^2/dt=-D\|\nabla c\|_2^2$. Stirring can increase the right-hand gradient norm while the patches remain sharply identifiable. As a result, frantic fine folding can precede rapid loss of visible contrast. The two stages are causally linked. The first has prepared the gradients that make the second fast. [Batchelor, small-scale variation of convected quantities](https://doi.org/10.1017/S002211205900009X)

**A change of pose inside a dissipative cycle.** A Stokes swimmer dissipates work over a stroke. Its [geometric displacement](../ideas/geometric-phase.md) depends on the oriented stroke and changes to its inverse if the stroke is reversed. Reversing the displacement costs still more work. One trajectory therefore contains both a reversible geometric record and positive accumulated dissipation. This is a particularly clear example of why assigning a single arrow to an entire effect can hide the interesting part.

## Clocks outside relaxation

A [wave attractor](../ideas/internal-wave-attractors.md) narrows in physical space while its waves acquire finer transverse scales. A [heteroclinic visit](../ideas/heteroclinic-switching.md) can be timed locally by an almost invisible outgoing amplitude. A [non-Abelian rung](../ideas/non-abelian-vortices.md) records the occurrence of a collision in the connectivity of the picture. Each gives evidence that time has passed. None supplies a general thermodynamic age.

For a short, baked sequence, the practical distinction is perceptual. A diffusing edge changes in every frame. A topology change supplies a single unmistakable before and after. A plasma echo needs an earlier rhythm, a quiet gap and a later event whose scale recalls the first two. Playback speed changes the duration of all three, while the number of relationships the viewer must recognize stays the same.

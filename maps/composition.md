# Ways to couple simple systems

Two patterns can cross and make a third visible feature. To distinguish the mechanisms, follow each pattern after the crossing. Does it keep its amplitude? Carry a dent away? Gain a new direction? Leave both lines connected by a third? These differences survive in a fixed bake. They give a collection of effects many different kinds of motion even if it draws on very few constituent systems.

## What passes between the parts?

| Relation | Mathematical distinction | What a viewer might see |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Superposition or shared readout | Independent states evolve, then enter the same observable. For coherent waves, intensity contains a cross term. | A slow envelope walks across two fast patterns. Dark seams move through the overlap. Both inputs emerge with the same amplitudes. [Dispersive revivals](../ideas/dispersive-revivals.md) are an extreme case of inherited phase structure. |
| One-way transport | One subsystem supplies the velocity or deformation map for another. The transported quantity can be passive. | A wide, slow eddy puts the same kink in many thin filaments. A bundle changes shape coherently even when the filaments have different colors or widths. [Topological stirring](../ideas/topological-stirring.md) |
| Parametric driving | One subsystem varies a coefficient in the other’s evolution, changing its stability or resonant response. | A nearly uniform vibration calls a faceted pattern out of a surface. In a subharmonic Faraday response the surface takes two drive periods to repeat. Its spatial pattern can be altogether absent from the driver. [Faraday pattern experiments](https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevE.52.R4606) |
| Resonant mode conversion | Nonlinear interaction exchanges amplitudes among phase-matched modes. | A third band grows in a new direction or at a new frequency while the original bands can weaken. Detuning makes contributions fall out of step. The emerging feature can survive separation of the inputs. [Armstrong et al., coupled waves](https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRev.127.1918) |
| Reciprocal phase feedback | Elements contribute to a field that in turn changes each element’s phase velocity. Interaction range and phase lag matter. | Neighbors settle into a shared beat while a nearby arc keeps slipping. One population supplies its own spatial contrast between order and drift. [Chimera states](../ideas/chimera-states.md) |
| Competition and shared supply | One mode suppresses another, or many locations draw from a common conserved quantity. The latter couples distant locations through a budget. | Competing rolls reveal a successor as a faint cross-grain. In a conserved bistable medium a sharp front slows and parks in empty space as it depletes the mobile pool. [Heteroclinic switching](../ideas/heteroclinic-switching.md) · [Mori, Jilkine & Edelstein-Keshet, wave-pinning](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/18212014/) |
| Coupling through geometry or connectivity | A shape cycle reconstructs pose, boundaries redirect propagation, or a shared field imposes compatibility at a collision. | A returning outline has drifted sideways, repeated reflections select a polygon, or two receding filaments acquire a crossbar. Each model restricts what the parts can do together. [Geometric phase](../ideas/geometric-phase.md) · [Internal-wave attractors](../ideas/internal-wave-attractors.md) · [Non-Abelian vortices](../ideas/non-abelian-vortices.md) |
| Coupling through history or a threshold | A hidden variable stores a trace, a slow parameter changes stability, or a switching event changes neighbors’ evolution. | A second disturbance releases a third pulse from apparent quiet. An arch lingers then snaps. A disappearing cell edge changes local growth rates. [Plasma echoes](../ideas/plasma-echoes.md) · [Delayed bifurcation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2010.07850) · [Foam coarsening](../ideas/foam-coarsening.md) · [Return-point memory](../ideas/return-point-memory.md) |

The rows can describe different parts of one evolution. Direction of influence, conservation, spatial range and memory are independent choices. For example, both a passive tracer and a parametrically driven surface receive one-way input. One inherits the driver’s deformation. The other can select a wavelength and beat of its own.

## Why a quiet state can precede a burst

**Transfer between decaying components.** An elementary feed-forward pair is $\dot x=-x$, $\dot y=Kx-y$. From $(1,0)$ the visible component is $y(t)=Kt e^{-t}$. Both eigenvalues are negative, yet a small upstream input can generate a large downstream excursion. If only the downstream variable is bright, the delay and burst come from the time required for transfer. In a spatial setting this has a counterpart in nonnormal transient amplification, where geometry and choice of observable control what grows. [Trefethen et al., hydrodynamic stability and transient growth](https://people.maths.ox.ac.uk/trefethen/ttrd.pdf)

**A limit of stability has already been crossed.** In delayed snap-through, slow driving carries a structure past a static threshold. The trajectory can still linger near the lost branch before accelerating away. The precursor might be a tiny change in curvature along exactly the direction in which the silhouette will later invert. An echo has a different kind of quiet. Its future pulse is already encoded in phase correlations despite a nearly flat spatial density. [Liu, Gomez & Vella](https://arxiv.org/abs/2010.07850) · [Temporal and spatial plasma wave echoes](https://authors.library.caltech.edu/records/qddf6-xjm06)

**The order of individually small motions matters.** Where deformation flows do not commute, a little loop of forward and reverse motions can leave a residual displacement. To leading order the residual scales with the loop area and a Lie bracket, up to the orientation convention. A player first sees the same local gestures seemingly cancel. The leftover rotation or translation reveals what their order accomplished. [Geometric phase](../ideas/geometric-phase.md)

## Two different kinds of economy

A volume full of filaments can inherit the same large-scale motion from one carrier field. Much of its apparent complexity resides in how many distinct markings reveal that one motion. A hard-edged network can be sparse yet expensive to describe by hand because every neighbor exchange changes future constraints. These are economies of description. The cost of a particular simulation or bake depends on its representation and desired resolution.

In two seconds, a relationship can survive through a recognizable feature. A crooked notch survives rephasing. A corner with an unusual angle marks a changed pose. A faint second orientation foreshadows a takeover. Statistical claims such as steadily lengthening residence times need several comparable events before they become perceptible. The short duration changes which *evidence* can be seen.
